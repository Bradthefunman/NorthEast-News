Privacy policy.

This informational policy describes how NorthEast News handles information in the current static workflow and reasonable future integrations.

Information you provide

If you submit a news tip, correction, contact message, sponsorship inquiry or newsletter signup, the information you choose to provide may be sent to the configured delivery provider for review or response. Do not submit passwords, financial credentials or information you are not authorized to share.

Newsletter and forms

Newsletter email addresses and form submissions are not stored in this GitHub repository. Until a delivery endpoint is configured, the site does not pretend to store or send them. Connected providers may have their own privacy policies and retention practices.

Analytics, cookies and external links

The current site does not require an analytics service or account cookies to read public stories. Future analytics, advertising or embedded services may use cookies or similar technologies and will be described here when activated. External websites have their own privacy policies.

Retention, children and updates

Information is retained only as reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was submitted or as required by the configured provider. The site is not directed to children. This policy may be updated as the site and its services change.

Questions

Use the contact form for privacy questions. This page is informational and is not legal advice.
