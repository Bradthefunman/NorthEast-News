A regional newsroom, built in public

NorthEast News is for people who want the region explained clearly.

We focus first on New Hampshire, then follow the stories that connect the rest of New England — with enough context to understand what happened and why it matters.

Our standards

Fast is useful. Accurate is essential.

We separate confirmed reporting from preliminary information, label developing stories, and keep the originating source attached to each published item. Short stories are written as original summaries; NorthEast News does not copy another publication’s article.

When the facts change, we update the story and preserve the publication history. If we make a meaningful error, we correct it clearly.

Coverage promise

NH first. New England always in view.

Our reporting lens includes state government, public safety, weather, business, technology, markets, communities and the everyday decisions that shape life across the region.
