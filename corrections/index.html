The published record

Corrections policy.

Accuracy matters after publication as much as before it.

How corrections work

Readers may submit a correction request with the article link or headline, the specific claim at issue and supporting information. Editors review requests against available evidence.

Updates and transparency

Factual errors are corrected in the article when warranted. Updated articles may show a new updated timestamp. Significant corrections may be identified in the story when that helps readers understand what changed.

Submit a correction

Use the news tip and correction form. A submission does not guarantee a change, and confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.
